## C16: where the overall CRPS gain comes from (official test)
Overall Δ = Σ (n_s/N) Δ_s; sunny/cloudy and ramp/non-ramp are separate, overlapping decompositions.

| Comparator | Overall Δ (kW) | Share from sunny | cloudy | ramp | non-ramp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E4-B-fit (direct expert) | −0.111 | **65%** | 35% | 15% | **85%** |
| E6-S (calm expert) | −0.084 | −1% | 101% | **133%** | −33% |
| G4 (4-feature gate) | −0.018 | −4% | 104% | **221%** | −121% |

- Against the **direct** expert, the gain is mostly **stable-condition distribution quality** (sunny, non-ramp), as the reviewer computed.
- Against the **calm** expert and **G4**, the gain is entirely on **ramps/cloudy** samples, partly offset by a small non-ramp cost.
- The combined statement: the framework keeps the calm expert's stable-condition quality and adds ramp robustness; it is not predominantly a ramp-forecasting advance relative to the direct expert.
