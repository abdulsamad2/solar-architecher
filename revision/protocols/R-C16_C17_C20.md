# Protocol R-C16: where the gain occurs; conditional intervals; strong comparators (C16, C17, C20)

Committed before any run. Labels: computed from the **frozen** test predictions; all new statistics are **post hoc**.

## C16 contribution analysis
For comparator X ∈ {E4-B-fit, G4, E6-S}: overall Δ = Σ_s (n_s / N) Δ_s for the partitions sunny/cloudy and ramp/non-ramp (separate, overlapping decompositions). Report n_s, Δ_s and share of the overall gain per stratum; also per test day.

## C17 conditional intervals
Raw (uncalibrated) and calibrated (frozen and past-only, R-C07) coverage, width and interval score at 50/80/90 for overall, sunny, cloudy, ramp, rising ramp, falling ramp, non-ramp, sunrise ramp; miss rates below/above. Statement: ramp strata are defined by the outcome, so conditional coverage is not covered by a marginal conformal guarantee.

## C20 comparators and inference
Paired test-day bootstrap (10,000, stratified sunny/cloudy, seed 20261022) of G3″-F − E6-S and G3″-F − G4 for every stratum; sample-weighted (pooled) and day-weighted (mean of daily means) summaries for all models.
